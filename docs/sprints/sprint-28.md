# Sprint 28 — The closing leaderboard

> Part of the [Ozark Open roadmap](../ROADMAP.md). **Post-freeze** (Andrew, Sept 7, 2026 — second dry run with Pat). **Write no new payout math.** `lib/payouts.ts:buildResultsTable()` already computes Pat's exact formula and returns his exact fields; this sprint is sorting, placement and the trigger. If a number on the new page disagrees with `lib/payouts.ts`, the new page is the bug.

**Goal:** when the book closes, the dashboard stops being a betting console and becomes the final standings — who won the most money, sortable, with the countdown and every live-tournament module gone.

**Target:** Sept 20, 2026 — last of the three, and **the safest to slip**: if it doesn't land, Pat presses the existing button on `/admin/close` and everyone reads `/results`, exactly as the pre-tournament checklist already says · **Blockers:** none hard. Land [26](sprint-26.md) and [27](sprint-27.md) first — 27 in particular, because it is the net this one gets to fall into.

**Reads:** `app/results/page.tsx` (the table being lifted, and the collection block's three deliberate properties), `lib/payouts.ts` (`buildResultsTable`, `cashReturned`, `finalizeReadiness`), `app/dashboard/page.tsx`, `app/api/admin/close/route.ts`, `docs/PRD.md` §5 (the pari-mutuel math) + §8.1 (why the finalize is guarded), `docs/PRE_TOURNAMENT_CHECKLIST.md` § Saturday night, `docs/sprints/sprint-25.md` (#108 — the guard).

### Why this sprint

Pat's words, plus his written amendment, which is source of truth for the schema:

> When phase 2 ends, we want to replace the pool total, entry, bets placed, place bets, house rules, alerts, how the pool works sections within the dashboard with a clear leaderboard ranking of who won the most money. Also clear out the countdown. This should only happen when pat uploads his spreadsheet for the final time to close phase 2 and resolve all bets. There should also be a "post leaderboard" button in the admin section that can do this directly as well.
>
> Schema for Sportsbook leaderboard: Rank (by profit/loss), Name, Entry Fee, Theoretical Payout, Actual Payout, Profit/Loss. The Sportsbook leaderboard after phase 2 closes should be sorted by profit/loss descending by default. But people should be able to click and filter based on other columns like entry fee, actual payout, etc.

The formula he wrote out is the one already shipped — `actual = theoretical(user) / Σtheoretical × pool` — and `/results` already renders his six columns. What's missing is that on Saturday night the app's front door still shows a pool total and a *Place Bets* button, and the thing everyone came for is on a page they have to be told about.

**Two decisions taken up front** (Andrew, Sept 7, 2026):

**One table, one truth.** The dashboard becomes the settlement surface and `/results` retires to a redirect. Two tables ranking the same 32 people by different columns is a thing to be argued about at 11pm, not a feature.

**The import offers the post; a human taps it.** Not fully automatic. Publishing everyone's payouts as a side effect of a file upload removes the one moment where somebody reads the numbers first — which is the entire reason `finalizeReadiness()` exists (Sprint 25 / #108). One tap after reading the import report is the right amount of friction, and Unpost is what makes it recoverable.

  - **Two naming traps.** "Leaderboard" is taken: `lib/leaderboard.ts` and `/leaderboard` are the *golf* standings, mirrored from a Google Sheet tab named, literally, "Sportsbook Leaderboard". Code names are `lib/standings.ts` / `standings-table.tsx`; the on-screen heading is **"Final Standings"**, and swapping that word later must not rename the modules. And **"Actual Payout" is not bare `actual`**: the Payout column shows `cashReturned(row) = actual + refunded`, which is #157 — a bettor with a $6 void read "$20 in → $10.00 back, −$4.00" and six dollars vanished off the page people open to find out what they're owed. Keep `cashReturned`, keep the "incl. $X refunded" sub-line, and `entry_fee + profit_loss === Payout` keeps reconciling on every row.
