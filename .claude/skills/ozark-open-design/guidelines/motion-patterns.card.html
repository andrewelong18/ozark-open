Hard rules
  • Transform and opacity only. Never top, margin, or an unbounded height. One exception: a progress bar's width — width is the meaning there, and scaleX squashes the pill cap.
  • Overshoot on transform only — on colour or opacity it overshoots past the token and can break contrast.
  • Never animate a display: contents element — it generates no box, so it silently does nothing.
  • Never gate an unmount behind motion-safe:. Anything that unmounts on animationend must still animate under reduced motion — which is why the floor is 0.01ms, never none.
  • Don't animate constantly-changing values — a 1Hz countdown or a field that recomputes per keystroke.
  • No infinite loops, no confetti, no gradient washes, no frosted glass. Sunlight readability wins.
