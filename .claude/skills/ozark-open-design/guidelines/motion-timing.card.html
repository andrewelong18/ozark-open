Easings — every curve is a named value

--ease-standard
Material 3 standard / emphasized · on-screen movement
--ease-out
Penner easeOutExpo · overlays, reveals
--ease-entrance
Material 3 emphasized-decelerate · content arriving
--ease-exit
Material 3 emphasized-accelerate · content leaving
--ease-overshoot
Penner easeOutBack, damped 1.56→1.35 · confirmation. Transform only.

Durations

--dur-fast120ms · hover, colour, press
--dur-base180ms · the default
--dur-slow260ms · dialog, backdrop, toast in
--dur-enter320ms · expressive — route & list arrival
--dur-exit160ms · exits leave fast

Productive (fast/base/slow) is the whole betting path — efficient, subtle, out of the way. Expressive (enter, overshoot) is arrival and confirmation only, rationed like brand gold: about one per screen. Nothing on the betting path exceeds --dur-slow.
